HOW FRACTIONAL CMO WORKS

A senior marketing leader for the price of an intern.

A fractional CMO gives your business experienced marketing leadership without the cost of a full-time executive. We begin with a structured onboarding period, establish an agreed direction, and manage the work through a consistent operating rhythm.

YOUR FIRST WEEKS WITH WIN-WIN

Here's how we get to work.

01 / DUE DILIGENCE & ONBOARDING Weeks 1–2

Audit your sales and marketing engine.

We examine how your business generates and converts opportunities, then assess your marketing strategy, performance, technology, team capabilities, and current spending. You receive a clear assessment of strengths, gaps, and immediate priorities.

02 / STRATEGY & FOUNDATIONS Weeks 3–4

Build a CEO-approved marketing strategy.

We strengthen your existing strategy or develop what is missing, including positioning, priority audiences, messaging, and a practical marketing roadmap. We align marketing priorities with your sales motion and secure CEO approval before major investments move forward.

03 / BUDGET & ACTIVATION Weeks 4–6

Set the marketing budget and priorities.

We establish an annual marketing budget covering advertising, trade shows, sales materials, signage, event marketing, and other approved initiatives. These costs sit outside the fractional CMO fee. With budgets and priorities agreed, we coordinate the resources and work needed to execute.

04 / ONGOING LEADERSHIP Week 6 onward

Lead execution and measure progress.

Your fractional CMO oversees approved work and meets with you weekly to review progress, address obstacles, and make decisions. Every quarter, we evaluate results against goals, review spending, and adjust the plan for the months ahead.

Typical timing shown. The schedule may vary based on the condition of the existing strategy, team readiness, and business complexity.
