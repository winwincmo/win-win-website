FOUNDER & AUTHOR

Amanda Moore Ortega.

I founded Win-Win to give growing B2B businesses access to experienced marketing leadership without the overhead of building a large internal team. After more than 25 years in brand strategy, digital marketing, and customer experience, I believe marketing works best when it understands how the sales team wins business.

EXPERIENCE & PERSPECTIVE

Connecting strategy to real business growth.

My work has taken me from digital agencies to in-house leadership roles, with a focus on positioning complex businesses, improving customer experiences, and making marketing more useful to the people responsible for revenue.

Before starting Win-Win, I served as Strategy Director at Liger Partners, where my work focused on marketing and brand strategy for B2B companies, including businesses navigating growth and change. Earlier, I led customer experience and SEO at Primerica Financial Services and worked in digital strategy and consulting roles, including Nurun, part of Publicis Groupe.

Across my career, I've worked on brand and digital initiatives involving organizations such as Coca-Cola, Lexus, Cox Communications, InterContinental Hotels Group, Merck, Pfizer, and the American Cancer Society. That mix of agency and in-house experience has shaped the way I think about customer needs, business priorities, and practical execution.

Today, I work with B2B businesses whose growth depends on relationships, trusted expertise, and strong sales teams. At Win-Win, fractional leadership sets the direction while specialized AI agents and workflows provide capacity to execute an approved plan. See how the engagement works.

THE WIN-WIN POINT OF VIEW

Marketing should earn its place in the growth plan.

For relationship-driven B2B businesses, marketing should reinforce the work of sales. That means understanding the customer, prioritizing what matters, investing responsibly, and building trust through clear, useful communication. AI can make the work more efficient, but people remain responsible for the strategy and the promises a business makes.
