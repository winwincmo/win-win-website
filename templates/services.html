A CLEAR SCOPE

What does the fee cover?

Your fractional CMO engagement covers an agreed scope of leadership and services. During onboarding, we assess the marketing function, strengthen the strategy where needed, and agree on the priorities and operating rhythm that will guide our work.

Marketing program expenses are budgeted separately so you know what you're paying for leadership and what you're investing in reaching customers.

Read about the first weeks of a Win-Win engagement.

Included in the agreed engagement

Fractional leadership, strategic planning, oversight, and the specific services defined in your scope of work. The level of ongoing execution support is established together.

Budgeted separately

Advertising and media, trade shows, event marketing, sales collateral, signage, and other approved outside expenses. Any additional tools or specialist services are scoped before investment.

A regular leadership rhythm

Weekly check-ins to address priorities and decisions, followed by quarterly reviews of performance, spending, and the plan ahead.
