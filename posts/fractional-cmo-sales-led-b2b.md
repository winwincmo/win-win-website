A referral gets your sales team in the door. The first call goes well. Then your contact needs to explain to their boss why your company belongs on the shortlist. They forward your website and look for a customer story that makes the case. What will they find?

That’s one of the first places I’d look if you asked me whether your sales-led B2B business needs a fractional CMO. You already know your team can build relationships. The question is whether your marketing carries those relationships forward between conversations.

## Build marketing around how you win business

Your best salespeople know what buyers need to hear before they feel comfortable moving forward. They know which customer story will land with an operations leader and what finance will ask before signing off. A fractional CMO can turn what those reps know into a marketing strategy the whole company can use.

Say you sell payment software to regional banks. Your contact likes the product, but the deal slows down every time implementation comes up. A fractional CMO might listen to sales calls and interview a customer who recently made the switch. That interview could become a case study showing how the bank rolled out the software in phases and what its team had to do at each step. Your rep can send it when the implementation question comes up. Your contact can pass it along to the people who have to approve the change.

Some of those people may never join a sales call. In a 2025 survey, 79% of U.S. decision-makers who influence purchases behind the scenes said they were more likely to advocate for an RFP proposal from a company that consistently publishes high-quality thought leadership. A practical guide to planning a bank’s payment software rollout gives them something concrete to discuss with colleagues. [2025 Edelman and LinkedIn B2B Thought Leadership Impact Report](https://www.edelman.com/expertise/Business-Marketing/2025-b2b-thought-leadership-report).

That guide can also reach buyers before the first conversation. In 6sense’s 2025 buyer research, 94% of buying groups ranked their preferred vendors before making contact. Even when someone refers you by name, what buyers find when they look you up can shape whether they take the meeting. [6sense’s 2025 Buyer Experience Report findings](https://6sense.com/news/the-timeline-for-influencing-b2b-buyers-is-shrinking-insights-from-6senses-2025-buyer-experience-report).

## What should a fractional CMO do first?

I’d start with a handful of recent deals. Look at why customers chose you and where promising conversations stalled. Ask your sales team what they keep having to explain from scratch. Then talk to buyers and see where their version of the story differs from yours.

That review should point to a specific place to start. If referral prospects take the first meeting but rarely advance, find out what they need before they’ll bring in another decision-maker. If your strongest customers come from one industry, make it easier for similar buyers to find you and see that you understand their business. [Read more about digital marketing for relationship-based sales](/blog/digital-marketing-relationship-based-sales/).

Agree with sales on how you’ll measure progress. If you want more referral prospects to reach a second meeting, track the share that do. If deals tend to stall after the proposal, ask recent buyers what gave their team enough confidence to move forward. In a long sales cycle, those signals give you something to learn from while revenue catches up.

## When does fractional leadership make sense?

A fractional CMO makes sense when marketing decisions keep landing on the founder or head of sales and the business needs someone to own those decisions every week. You might already have a coordinator or an agency doing good work. A fractional CMO gives that work direction and decides where to put the budget based on what buyers need and where sales sees room to grow.

A project specialist can be a good fit when you need a specific deliverable, like a new website. If you’re still deciding which customers to pursue and how to make your case to them, start with someone who can own that strategy and see it through.
